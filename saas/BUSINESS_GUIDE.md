- Basic model (3B parameters)

- **Target:** Very early startups, MVP builders

### Tier 2: Professional ($1,500)
- 5,000 training examples
- 1 week delivery
- Advanced model (7B parameters)
- Ollama + API wrapper
- Priority support
- **Target:** Growing startups, 10-50 employees

### Tier 3: Enterprise ($5,000)
- 20,000 training examples
- 3-5 days delivery
- Premium model (13B parameters)
- Full deployment + integration
- Ongoing updates (3 months)
- **Target:** Established companies, 50+ employees

## Sales Process

### Step 1: Lead Generation

**Where to find startups:**
- Y Combinator directory (https://www.ycombinator.com/companies)
- Product Hunt (launching startups)
- IndieHackers community
- LinkedIn (search "founder" + "AI")
- Twitter/X (search #startup #AI)
- Startup Discord servers

**Cold email template:**
```
Subject: Custom AI for [Their Niche] - 100% Private & Offline

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company] is building [product] in [niche].

We help startups like yours get their own private AI expert trained specifically on [their domain] - no cloud costs, runs offline, yours forever.

Unlike ChatGPT or Claude:
- Trained only on YOUR niche
- Runs on your hardware (zero API costs)
- 100% private (your data never leaves)
- Expert-level responses in your domain

Cost: $500-5000 depending on size
Time: 1-2 weeks delivery

Want to see a demo?

[Your Name]
```

### Step 2: Demo/Discovery Call

**What to show:**
1. Your admin dashboard
2. Sample training data (high quality)
3. Example model outputs
4. Cost comparison (vs OpenAI/Claude)
5. Privacy benefits

**Questions to ask:**
- What's your niche/domain?
- What AI features do you need?
- What's your current AI spend?
- Privacy concerns?
- Timeline?

### Step 3: Onboarding

1. Get their topics/niche (15-20 topics)
2. Estimate examples needed
3. Quote price
4. Sign agreement
5. Get 50% upfront

### Step 4: Delivery

1. Onboard in your dashboard
2. Generate training data (automated)
3. Send them data + Colab notebook
4. They train (or you train for them)
5. Package & deliver model
6. Get remaining 50%

### Step 5: Upsell

Offer:
- Model updates ($200/month)
- More training data ($100/1000 examples)
- Integration help ($500)
- Custom features ($1000+)
